**Project lead: Jincheng Li (李锦承).** The author defined the project requirements, assembled and operated the commercial hardware, directed AI-assisted implementation, supplied the self-recorded CSV files and photographs, and developed the explanatory material with AI assistance. See [AI and contributions](docs/AI_AND_CONTRIBUTIONS.md).

Public-facing asset names replace the original messaging-service filenames; selected image pixels are unchanged. The data manifest records SHA-256 hashes of the raw CSV files.
